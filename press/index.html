Description

Short: The Black Wave killed every engine. One truck still runs. Drive 500 km north to Haven-01 in a crew of up to six: scavenge dead towns, turn your rig into a home on wheels, fight raiders, and stay ahead of the wave that rises every night.

The Last Truck is an isometric co-op survival game about a road trip at the end of the world. After the Black Wave killed every engine on the continent, one old truck still runs: yours. Haven-01, the last safe settlement, lies 500 kilometres to the north, and every night the Wave rises in the south and rolls after you.

Your truck is your base. Build a workbench, bunks and a medical cabinet into the trailer, upgrade its armour, suspension, fuel tanks, cargo, radar, winch and roof defences, and keep its battery, belts, radiator, fuel line and tyres alive. Stop in dead towns to search gas stations, shops, houses and farms, craft what you need at the truck, and get back before dark.

Play solo or with up to five friends. Take a seat as driver, gunner, spotter or mechanic. Raiders own the roads by day; at night the Burned wake inside the Wave and hunt by sound. And between the storms there is time for darts, backgammon, basketball, a campfire, and a stray cat that might adopt your crew.

Features

  • Isometric co-op road survival for 1 to 6 players
  • A truck that is your base: build rooms, upgrade parts, repair what breaks
  • Hunger, thirst, fatigue, wounds and bandages
  • Skills that grow by doing: a rookie driver slides around, a veteran does not
  • Dense towns full of buildings you can enter and loot
  • Random road events, radio calls and strangers who need help
  • Raiders by day, the Burned by night, the Black Wave always behind you
  • Darts, backgammon, basketball, football, and a cat to pet
