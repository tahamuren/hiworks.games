About the game

Drive 500 km north to Haven-01 with up to five friends. Scavenge dead towns, turn your rig into a home on wheels, fight raiders, and stay ahead of the wave that rises every night.

One truck. 500 kilometres. The Black Wave behind you.

The Black Wave killed every engine on the continent, all but yours. Haven-01, the last safe settlement, lies 500 km to the north. Every night the Wave rises in the south and rolls after you. Keep moving, or be swallowed.

Your truck is your home

Build a workbench, bunks and a medical cabinet into the trailer. Upgrade the armour, suspension, fuel tanks, cargo space, radar, winch and roof defences, but every upgrade has a cost: more armour means more weight and more fuel. Keep it alive too. The battery, belts, radiator, fuel line and tyres all wear out.

Search every building

Gas stations, shops, houses, farms: every door opens. Turn the view to see inside, search the shelves and carry home what you can. At the truck's workbench, craft tools, weapons, ammunition, medkits and pipe bombs.

Solo, or with up to five friends

Play alone or in online co-op for up to six players. Take a seat in the truck as driver, gunner, spotter or mechanic, split up to loot a town, and get back to the truck before dark.

The living are worse

Raiders own the roads. Fight them with rifles, shotguns and grenades, or find another way round. At night the Burned wake inside the Wave. They hunt by sound, so keep quiet, or make it loud and run.

Live a little

Between the storms there is time to breathe: darts and backgammon in the trailer, a basketball hoop behind a gas station, a football match in a farmyard, a campfire under the awning, and a stray cat who might just adopt your crew.
